The problem

The same rule on paper, with two different outcomes.

A $100,000 evaluation at two firms. Both allow a 6% max drawdown. Drag the slider, or press play.

Where accounts really fail

The profit target is rarely what stops you.

Evaluations are often lost to a rule the trader read once, quickly, on a pricing page. MyPropBuddy reads these lines for you.

Evaluation rules (example)

  1. Maximum drawdown is 6%, trailing from the highest equity reached, including open profit.
  2. Daily loss of 5%, measured from the higher of balance or equity at the start of the day.
  3. No single day may make up more than 40% of total profit.
  4. Trading within 2 minutes of high-impact news is not permitted on funded accounts.
  5. A minimum of 5 trading days is required to pass.
  6. Expert advisors and copy trading require written approval.

How it works

From how you trade to a shortlist, in three steps.

  1. Tell it how you trade

    Chat with it, or fill in a short form: your markets, style, budget, how long you hold trades and how you handle news.

  2. It scores every program

    A tested calculator checks each funding program against your style on ten points. Answers you leave blank are reported as unknown, never guessed.

  3. You see your best fits

    Ranked programs, in plain language, with the one rule most likely to end your account and the source for every fact.

What it checks

Ten points of fit, weighted by what costs traders the most.

The bar shows how much each check counts toward a program's score.

  • Max drawdown modelStatic, trailing or end-of-day, and when the floor stops moving.
  • Strategy restrictionsNews trading, expert advisors, copy trading, holding over weekends.
  • Daily loss limitHow it's measured, from balance or equity, and when the day resets.
  • Consistency rulesCaps on how much of your profit one day can make up.
  • Payout termsProfit split, first payout timing and how often you can withdraw.
  • Profit target vs riskWhether the target is realistic inside the room the rules give you.
  • Time limits and minimum daysDeadlines, and the trading days you must log to pass.
  • Cost and affordabilityThe evaluation fee against the budget you have now.
  • Firm track recordHow reliably the firm pays out.
  • Platform, markets and accessYour trading platform, your instruments and your country.

Why trust it

The AI asks the questions. Tested code makes the call.

An AI answering rule questions from memory will confidently quote a drawdown type that changed last week. MyPropBuddy keeps the AI to the conversation.

  • The AI never ranks firms and never states a rule from memory.
  • Every recommendation comes from the same tested calculator, so the same answers always give the same result.
  • Every rule is stored with its source, a quote from the firm and the date it was checked.
  • Unchecked data is labelled, and it lowers the confidence of any match that relies on it.

FAQ

Questions before you join.

Why not ask ChatGPT which firm to pick?

A general AI answers from memory, and prop firms change their rules often. MyPropBuddy only states rules from its own catalogue, each with a source and a date.

Which firms does it cover?

Forex prop firms first. Futures firms come next, since the rule model already handles their trailing drawdowns and dollar loss limits.

What if I don't know the answer to a question?

Leave it blank. MyPropBuddy tells you what it couldn't judge without that answer, and never fills the gap with a guess.

When can I use it?

When the firm rules are checked. People on the waitlist get the first invites.

Is this financial advice?

No. MyPropBuddy compares published rules against how you say you trade. The decision is yours.
